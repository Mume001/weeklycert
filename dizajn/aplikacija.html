What an OT code means

B
Time and a half after 8 hours in a day.
B2
Time and a half after 40 hours in a week.
E2
Saturday may be a straight-time make-up day when a day was lost to weather.
O
Double time on Saturday, Sunday and holidays.

Codes come from the OVERTIME PAGE of your wage schedule. A classification usually carries several; we apply all of them and take the highest premium for each hour.

Holidays

needs you once

Codes O and R depend on which days count as holidays on this schedule. That list is on the HOLIDAY PAGE of your PRC schedule and differs by trade.

Until this is set, a day marked as a holiday raises a warning instead of computing a premium.
